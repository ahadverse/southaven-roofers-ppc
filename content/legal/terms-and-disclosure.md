## Homeowner responsibilities

You are responsible for deciding whether to hire any contractor. Before you sign anything or pay anyone, we strongly encourage you to:

- **Verify the license.** The [Mississippi State Board of Contractors](https://www.msboc.us/) requires a state license for residential roofing jobs over **$10,000**. Look up the contractor's license on the board's website, and ask whether a local permit is needed and who will pull it.
- **Confirm insurance.** Ask for current certificates of general liability and workers' compensation insurance, and call the insurance company listed if you want to confirm the coverage is active.
- **Check references and reviews.** Ask for recent local jobs you can look at and people you can call.
- **Get written estimates.** An estimate should spell out the scope of work, materials, price, payment schedule, timeline, cleanup and warranty terms. Getting more than one estimate is often worthwhile.
- **Use a written contract.** Get a signed contract before work begins, read it fully, never sign anything with blank spaces, and be cautious about large up-front payments.
- **Protect your insurance claim.** Read any document related to your insurance claim carefully before signing it, and ask your insurance agent or company if you are unsure what it means.
