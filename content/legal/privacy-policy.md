This Privacy Policy explains what information {{siteName}} collects when you visit {{siteUrl}} or call the phone number shown on the site, how that information is used and shared, and the choices you have. We have tried to keep it short and in plain English.

**The short version:**

- The website has **no forms, no accounts, no newsletter and no cookies of its own**. You can read every page without telling us who you are.
- The phone number on the site is a **call-tracking number run by a third-party call-routing provider**. When you call, your call is connected to an **independent local roofing contractor**, and your caller information goes to that provider and that contractor.
- **Calls may be recorded** by the call-routing provider and/or the contractor.
- We are **paid when calls are connected**. See our [Terms of Use & Advertising Disclosure](/terms-and-disclosure) for details.
- Privacy questions go to **{{contactEmail}}**, not to the phone number.

## Who we are

{{siteName}} is a free referral service for homeowners in Southaven, Mississippi, and the surrounding DeSoto County area. The site publishes roofing information and a phone number. When you call that number, you are connected with an independent, participating local roofing contractor. {{siteName}} does not perform roofing work and does not employ the contractors who answer calls.

In this policy:

- **"We," "us" and "our"** mean the operator of {{siteName}}.
- **"Call-routing provider"** means the third-party pay-per-call and call-tracking network that supplies the phone number shown on the site and connects calls to contractors.
- **"Participating contractor"** means an independent roofing business that receives calls through that network. Each contractor is a separate company, responsible for its own handling of your information.

## Information we collect

### When you visit the website

The site does not ask you to type in your name, address, email or phone number. There are no contact forms, accounts, comment sections or newsletter sign-ups. The only information collected during a visit is technical:

- **Server logs.** Like nearly every website, our hosting provider (Vercel) automatically records basic details when your browser requests a page, such as your **IP address**, your **browser and device type** (the "user agent"), the **pages requested**, the date and time, and the referring page if your browser sends one.
- **Aggregated analytics.** We use Vercel Web Analytics, a cookieless analytics tool, to count page views and to record when a **call button is clicked** (which button and on which page). We also keep our own simple log of call-button taps. Details are in [Cookies and analytics](#cookies-and-analytics) below.

### When you call the phone number

The phone number on the site is a tracking number provided by a third-party call-routing provider. When you call it, the provider and the participating contractor who answers receive and process information such as:

- **Your phone number and other caller ID information**
- **The date, time and length of the call**
- **A general location based on your phone number** (for example, the area code or region), which helps route your call to a contractor who serves your area
- **A recording of the call**, where calls are recorded (see the [call recording notice](#call-recording-notice))
- **Anything you choose to say during the call**, such as your name, address, details about your roof or an insurance claim, and the best time to reach you

We may be able to see reports from the call-routing provider about calls made through the site, such as call dates, times, durations, general locations and whether a call was connected. In some cases these reports can include the calling number or a recording. We use them only to confirm that calls are routed and credited correctly.

### When you email us

If you email us at {{contactEmail}}, we receive your email address, your name if you include it, and whatever you write. Please do not send sensitive information such as Social Security numbers, bank account details or payment card numbers by email.

## How information is used

Information collected through the site and calls is used to:

- **Connect your call** to a participating contractor who serves your area
- **Let the contractor help you**, for example by calling you back, scheduling a visit or preparing an estimate
- **Handle billing and disputes.** The call-routing provider and participating contractors use call records (and sometimes recordings) to decide whether a call qualifies for payment and to resolve disagreements about calls. This is part of how {{siteName}} is compensated.
- **Check call quality and train staff**, which the call-routing provider and contractors may do with recordings
- **Run, secure and improve the website**, such as seeing which pages are useful, fixing errors and blocking abuse
- **Answer your emails**
- **Meet legal obligations**, enforce our terms and protect the rights and safety of visitors, contractors and ourselves

We do not use your information to show you targeted ads, and the site does not track you across other websites.

## How information is shared

### With the call-routing provider

The call-routing provider supplies the phone number, connects your call, may record it and keeps records of calls for routing, billing and dispute purposes. It handles your information under its own privacy policy and practices.

### With the participating contractor

The contractor who answers your call receives your phone number and caller ID information, hears what you tell them, and may record the call. The contractor is an independent business, not controlled by us. Its use of your information (for example, follow-up calls, texts, estimates and records of any job) is governed by its own practices and privacy policy, so ask the contractor if you have questions about how it handles your information.

### With hosting, analytics and email providers

- **Vercel** hosts the website, keeps server logs and provides Vercel Web Analytics. You can read [Vercel's privacy notice](https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy) for how it handles data.
- **MongoDB Atlas** stores the website's content and the call-button tap log described below.
- **Our email provider** stores and delivers messages you send to {{contactEmail}}.

These providers handle information on our behalf so the site can operate.

### For legal reasons

We may disclose information if we believe in good faith that it is required by law, subpoena, court order or other legal process, or that it is necessary to protect the rights, property or safety of anyone, to prevent fraud (including fraudulent calls) or to enforce our terms.

### If the business changes hands

If the site or business is sold, merged or transferred, the information we hold may be transferred to the new owner, who would be expected to follow this policy or provide notice of any changes.

### A note about "sales" of personal information

We do not rent or sell visitor lists, and we do not give the information we hold to data brokers or advertisers. However, our business is paid when your call is connected to a contractor, and your caller information goes to that contractor so they can help you. Some state privacy laws define "sale" or "sharing" very broadly, so this arrangement may count as a sale or sharing under those laws. If you do not want your caller information shared this way, the simplest choice is not to call the number and to contact a roofing contractor directly instead.

## Call recording notice

**Calls to the phone number on this site may be recorded and monitored** by the call-routing provider and/or the participating contractor for quality, training, billing and dispute purposes. By calling the number, you understand and agree that your call may be recorded.

A recording may capture anything said during the call, including your name, address and details about your home. Recordings are kept and controlled by the call-routing provider and the contractor under their own policies. If you do not want your call recorded, please do not call the number; you can find and contact a roofing contractor on your own instead.

## Cookies and analytics

**{{siteName}} does not set its own cookies.** The site does not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, social media plugins or remarketing tools. Images and fonts are served from the site itself, so loading a page does not send requests to Google Fonts or similar outside font or image services.

We use **Vercel Web Analytics**, which is designed to work **without cookies**. According to Vercel, it identifies visits using a hash created from the incoming request, discards that visit information after 24 hours, and gives us **aggregated statistics** rather than information tied to an individual. The kinds of data it records include:

- The page viewed and the referring website
- The date and time
- General location (such as country, state or city)
- Device type, operating system and browser
- Our custom **"call button clicked"** event, which records which call button was tapped and on which page. It does not record your phone number or anything about the call itself.

We also keep **our own log of call-button taps** in the site’s database (hosted with MongoDB Atlas), so we can see which pages and buttons help homeowners reach a roofer. Each entry records only which button was tapped, the page it was on, the general device type (phone, tablet or computer), the referring website if any, and the time. It does **not** store your IP address, phone number, name, cookies or anything about the call itself.

The site’s private admin area uses a login cookie for the site owner only. Visitors never receive it.

Because the site does not track you across other websites or use advertising cookies, there is no cross-site tracking for browser settings like Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control to switch off. Our hosting provider may use technical security measures (for example, to block automated attacks) that work independently of us.

## Data retention

We keep information only as long as it is reasonably needed for the purposes described in this policy, or longer if the law requires it. In general:

- **Server logs and analytics** are kept by Vercel for limited periods under its own retention practices. Analytics reports are aggregated.
- **Call records and recordings** are kept by the call-routing provider and the participating contractor under their own retention policies, which may include keeping them for billing, dispute and legal purposes.
- **Emails** you send us are kept as long as needed to respond and to keep reasonable records, then deleted.

## Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information, including encrypted (HTTPS) connections to the site and reputable service providers, and we limit who can access call reports and email. No website, phone system or method of storage is completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security. Please avoid sending sensitive personal or financial information by email.

## Your choices and rights

- **Browse without sharing.** You can read everything on the site without giving us any personal information.
- **Choose whether to call.** Calling is optional. If you do not want your caller information shared with a contractor or your call recorded, do not call the number. You can look up and contact a roofer yourself, for example by checking licensed contractors through the [Mississippi State Board of Contractors](https://www.msboc.us/).
- **Stop contractor follow-up.** If a contractor contacts you after your call and you no longer want to hear from them, tell the contractor directly and ask to be added to its do-not-call list.
- **Ask about your information.** You can email {{contactEmail}} to ask what information we hold about you, or to ask us to correct or delete it. Because call records are held mainly by the call-routing provider and the contractor, we will also help point you to the right company for call-related requests. To find records connected to a call, we may ask for the phone number you called from and the approximate date and time of the call, and we may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

**Residents of some states may have additional rights.** Depending on where you live (for example, California, Colorado, Connecticut, Tennessee, Virginia and a growing number of other states), privacy laws may give you rights such as the right to know what personal information is collected, to access, correct or delete it, to opt out of its "sale" or "sharing," and to appeal a decision about your request. Whether a particular law applies depends on the law and on the size and type of business involved. To make a request, email **{{contactEmail}}** with "Privacy Request" in the subject line. You may use an authorized agent where the law allows it. We will respond within the time required by applicable law, and we will not treat you differently for exercising your privacy rights.

If you believe a child has provided personal information through the site or a call, email {{contactEmail}} and we will take reasonable steps to delete it and to ask the call-routing provider and contractor to do the same.
